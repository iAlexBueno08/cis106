# Linux Commands

## mkdir
**Definition:** Creates a new directory.  
**Usage/Formula:** `mkdir [directory_name]` `mkdir -p directory/directory(For parent directory)` 
**Examples:**  
- `mkdir projects`  
- `mkdir /home/user/documents/new_folder`
- `mkdir -p /home/user/movie/horror`

## touch
**Definition:** Creates an empty file.  
**Usage/Formula:** `touch [file_name]`  
**Examples:**  
- `touch notes.txt`  
- `touch /home/user/scripts/run.sh`

## rm
**Definition:** Removes files or directories.  
**Usage/Formula:** `rm [options] [file_or_directory]` `rm -r [directory_everythin_in_it]` 
**Examples:**  
- `rm file.txt`  
- `rm -r old_folder`

## cp
**Definition:** Copies files or directories from one location to another.  
**Usage/Formula:** `cp [options] [source] [destination]`  
**Examples:**  
- `cp file.txt /home/user/backup/`  
- `cp -r folder1/ folder2/`

## mv
**Definition:** Moves or renames files and directories.  
**Usage/Formula:** `mv [source] [destination]`  
**Examples:**  
- `mv file.txt /home/user/documents/`  
- `mv oldname.txt newname.txt`
